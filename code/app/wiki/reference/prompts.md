Copy a prompt into the Code tab of the Claude desktop app and change the parts
in brackets. Each one asks for one change, which is how you get good results:
one change, check it in the browser, then the next. For why these work, see
[How to ask Claude](/wiki/guides/ask-claude).

## Start and find your way

- "Start my work app and open it in the browser."
- "Is my app running? If not, start it on port 9950."
- "Give me a tour of my app: what each module in the sidebar is for, in one line each."
- "Which modules do I have, and which block is each one built on?"
- "What changed in my app since yesterday?"

## About: your role

- "Interview me to fill in About. Ask one question at a time."
- "Update About: my team is now [Customer Success, mid-size accounts]."
- "Read About and tell me what is missing for someone new to understand my role."

## People

- "Add these people to People: [paste names, roles and organisations]."
- "Add Maya Okafor, head of operations at Tallow & Co. We met at the handover in September."
- "Set last contact for [Daniel Reyes] to today."
- "Which people have I not been in touch with for more than a month?"
- "Add to [Maya]'s notes: [wants the renewal proposal a week before the business review]."

## Tasks

- "Add a followup for [Maya Okafor]: [send the renewal proposal], due [Tuesday]."
- "Turn these notes into tasks, one per action, with a person and a due date where you can tell: [paste notes]."
- "Mark [switch on single sign-on for Harbourline] as blocked, waiting on [Lena Hoffmann]."
- "Which tasks are overdue?"
- "Move every task due this week that is still in the backlog to in progress."

## Every day

- "Start of day: what is due today and this week, what is blocked, and who am I waiting on?"
- "End of day: here is what I did today: [notes]. Update Tasks and People to match."
- "What did I agree to in [the Tallow & Co business review]? Make a task for each commitment."
- "Prepare me for my meeting with [Tallow & Co]: the people, the open tasks and the notes."

## Goals

- "Add a goal: [renewals closed], start [5], target [9] by [end of Q4]. Owner [me]."
- "Set [healthy accounts] to [19]. It is our monthly check-in."
- "Which of my goals are off track, and what would it take to get each back on pace?"
- "Move the target date of [days to go live] to [Q1 2027], and set its start to where it stands today."

## Change a module

- "Add a [Next step] column to Tasks, one line of text."
- "Rename the [Organisation] column in People to [Company]."
- "Move [Last contact] to be the second column in People."
- "Remove the [Role] column from People. Keep the data in the file."
- "Show Tasks as a table instead of a task list."
- "Rename the People module to [Contacts]."
- "Move Tasks below People in the sidebar."
- "Change the hint on [Due] in Tasks to [the date you promised it]."

## Build a module

- "I want a module for my customer accounts. Ask me what columns it needs, then build it on the datatable block."
- "Add an Accounts module with these columns: [account, plan, renewal date, health, main contact, notes]."
- "Add these accounts to Accounts: [Tallow & Co, Harbourline Logistics, Fernwood Studio]."
- "Add a Projects module on the datatable block, in the task view."
- "Show me the module file and the data file you made for [Accounts], and explain each part."

## Bring in data

- "Here is an export from Excel: [paste rows]. Put them in [Accounts], one row per line."
- "Read [the CSV file I put in the folder] and add its rows to [People]. Show me the first three before you write them all."
- "Here is an email: [paste]. What should go into Tasks or People?"

## Wiki

- "Write a wiki page from these meeting notes: [paste]. Put it in a folder called [accounts]."
- "Add a wiki page on how our quarterly business reviews run."
- "Tidy up [this page] using the wiki's writing rules."
- "What does the wiki say about [conflicts]?"

## Checkpoints and undo

- "Save a checkpoint called [Accounts module added]."
- "Undo your last change."
- "Go back to the checkpoint from [this morning]. Tell me what will be lost first."
- "List my checkpoints from this week."

## When something breaks

- "The page is blank. Find out why and fix it."
- "I see this error: [paste the error]. What does it mean, and can you fix it?"
- "My save was refused as a conflict. What happened?"
- "Show me what you changed before I keep it."

## Learn and grow

- "Explain what you changed, in plain words, and what I should check on screen."
- "What is one next thing I could build that would save me time each week?"
- "Which modules can I add from the kit?"
- "Is there a newer kit? Take it, and walk me through anything I need to merge."
- "Write a skill for my [Monday account review], so you do it the same way every week."
- "Add a rule to CLAUDE.md: [always ask before deleting a row]."
