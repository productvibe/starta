This is your work app: a custom app for your own job. It keeps one set of data
about your work, with a structured screen on top, and Claude keeps it current.
You own it, run it, build it and use it.

You build it by talking to Claude in the Code tab of the Claude desktop app.
You describe what you want, Claude changes the code and the data, and you check
the result in your browser. You do not write React and you do not type
commands.

## Your first hour

1. Read [What a work app is](/wiki/idea), about five minutes.
2. Follow [Learn](/wiki/learn) from the setup page through lesson 7.
3. By the end you have a running app with your own role, people and tasks, a
   module you designed, and something you will open tomorrow.

## Your wiki, too

This wiki ships with every work app, and it is yours to change. Keep what you
learn about your own work here, in folders of your own. Ask Claude, for
example: "Add a wiki page about how our business reviews run." The rules for
writing pages are in [Writing pages](/wiki/about/ai-instructions).
